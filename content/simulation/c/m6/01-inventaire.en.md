On 31 December: count, depreciate, provide [[syscohada:6031]].

### Thursday 31 December: the count

On the 31st Linda and Patrice count the TV sets, units and small appliances in store and value them at the year's average cost.

[[piece:S-C-12-010]]

**The rule.** Closing stock in 3111, 3112, 3121, through 6031.

> **Why this choice and not another.** WAC smooths the importer's quarterly rises; FIFO would give a higher stock.

**What it changes.** Closing stock: 8,835,000 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Count sheets in the Inventory binder, summary in the inventory book. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M20 “Inventories at year end”.

### Thursday 31 December: depreciate and provide

Patrice depreciates the tools over five years. Nine customers reported in December a failure on units installed since November, still under warranty: he estimates each repair at 50,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-C-12-011]]

**The rule.** Charge 6813, depreciation 2841; provision 6911 against 192.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why is this provision deductible, unlike Ngono Mode's litigation provision? Here the charge is precise (nine costed repairs), probable (failures reported) and booked: the CGI's three conditions are met.

**What it changes.** 770,000 FCFA of year-end charges.

**Where it is filed.** Asset register and failure list in the Fixed-asset binder. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M22 “Provisions for risks and charges”.

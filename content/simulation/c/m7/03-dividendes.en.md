June to August 2027: the meeting, then the dividends [[cgi:art. 70]].

### Friday 25 June 2027: the partners' meeting

Mr Kamga and Mrs Eyenga approve the 2026 accounts. Out of the 10,880,195 FCFA profit they fund the legal reserve with 400,000 FCFA, distribute 6,000,000 FCFA and carry the rest forward.

[[piece:S-C-18-001]]

**The rule.** Meeting within six months of year end; at least one tenth of profit funds the legal reserve, up to one fifth of capital: 131 against 111, 465 and 121.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why only 400,000 FCFA? The reserve stops at one fifth of capital, 400,000 FCFA. Dividends are not an expense: they share a profit already taxed.

**What it changes.** A debt to the partners (465).

**Where it is filed.** Signed minutes in the permanent file. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M25 “Closing, reopening and appropriation”.

### Friday 9 July 2027: paying the dividends

Patrice transfers the net dividends (5,010,000 FCFA) after the 990,000 FCFA withholding. Mr Kamga receives 80% of the net.

[[piece:S-C-19-001]]

**The rule.** 15% withholding plus council cents: 465 against 4478 and 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The company withholds its partners' tax: it is their tax, not the company's.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by the net.

**Where it is filed.** Transfer notice in the Bank binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Friday 13 August 2027: paying over the withholding tax

Before the 15th of the next month, Patrice pays the withholding over to the tax office.

[[piece:S-C-20-001]]

**The rule.** 4478 cleared by 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Keeping the withholding would keep the State's money.

**What it changes.** The tax debt is cleared.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Tax returns binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audit”.

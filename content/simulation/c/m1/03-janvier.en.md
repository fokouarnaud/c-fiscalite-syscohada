January 2026: the shop opens in Bonamoussadi and the workshop equips itself. Read each scene, then record the entry in the quiz [[cgi:art. 142]].

### Friday 2 January: the capital

Mr Kamga and Mrs Eyenga pay 2,000,000 FCFA into an account in the name of the company being formed, blocked until registration.

[[piece:S-C-01-006]]

**The rule.** Capital 1013, bank 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Being a partner does not make Mr Kamga a manager: as a civil servant he holds shares but entrusts the management to Patrice, a third party.

**What it changes.** The bank is funded; no revenue.

**Where it is filed.** Permanent file. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Accounting M3 “Assets and liabilities”.

### Monday 5 January: restocking at the importer

Mr Essomba delivers 20 TV sets, 15 air conditioners and 40 small appliances.

[[piece:S-C-01-011]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011, VAT 4452, 2% withholding 4492.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Appliances stay in purchases (6011) all year; stock is counted on 31 December. The withholding is an advance on tax, not a cost.

**What it changes.** Debt to the importer: 10,124,375 FCFA, payable at the end of next month.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Purchases binder with delivery notes signed by Linda. Journal AC.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”.

### Tuesday 6 January: the notary's invoice

With two million of capital the articles go through Maître Moukoko. Once the company is registered, Patrice pays the invoice.

[[piece:S-C-01-007]]

**The rule.** Fees 6324, duties 646, VAT 4454.

> **Why this choice and not another.** No capitalised formation expenses: revised SYSCOHADA expenses them.

**What it changes.** An expense in the first month.

**Where it is filed.** Permanent file. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

### Tuesday 6 January: the rent

Patrice pays Mr Ngando the shop rent, less 25,000 FCFA withheld for tax.

[[piece:S-C-01-010]]

**The rule.** Gross rent 6222, withholding 4478.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The expense is gross rent; the withholding is a debt to the State.

**What it changes.** The withholding joins the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Bank binder; lease in the permanent file. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Wednesday 7 January: the installation tools

To install units properly, the workshop buys a refrigerant recovery unit, a vacuum pump and drills: 1,600,000 FCFA before tax.

[[piece:S-C-01-008]]

**The rule.** Industrial tools 2412, VAT on fixed assets 4451, fixed-asset supplier 4812, paid at 30 days.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Above 500,000 FCFA before tax, tools go on the balance sheet: they are depreciated, not expensed at once.

**What it changes.** Assets grow; the VAT creates a credit on the February return.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Fixed-asset binder; the tools join the asset register. Journal AC.

**See again:** Accounting M13 “Fixed assets”.

### Tuesday 20 January: the month's installations

Joël and Rodrigue installed 14 units at Hôtel Les Palmiers de Kotto (fictif). Patrice invoices the installation, payable on the 10th of next month.

[[piece:S-C-01-012]]

**The rule.** Services sold 706, VAT charged 4431, customer 411.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why 706 and not 7011? The unit is goods sold; installing it is work, a service.

**What it changes.** A 584,325 FCFA receivable.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice copy in the Sales binder with the job sheets signed by the customer. Journal VT.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Saturday 31 January: the counter

Linda sold 5,976,810 FCFA incl. tax at the counter, in cash.

[[piece:S-C-01-013]]

**The rule.** Cash 5711, sales 7011, VAT 4431.

> **Why this choice and not another.** VAT collected does not belong to the shop.

**What it changes.** Turnover grows.

**Where it is filed.** Daily cash book pages and summary in the Cash binder. Journal CA.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Saturday 31 January: the Orange Money statement

Customers paying by phone paid 5,719,230 FCFA; the operator takes its fees and the balance goes to the bank.

[[piece:S-C-01-014]]

**The rule.** 552, fees 631, 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Fees are a bank service, not a discount.

**What it changes.** The transfer reaches the bank.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Journal OM.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Saturday 31 January: payroll

Patrice prepares the four payslips, his own included, and transfers 634,929 FCFA.

[[piece:S-C-01-015]]

**The rule.** Gross 6611, levies, withholdings, net 422.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The non-partner manager is an employee; the civil-servant partner is not.

**What it changes.** Withholdings join the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Signed payslips in the Payroll binder. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M11 “Payroll”.

### Saturday 31 January: the cash deposits

Every week Linda takes the takings to the bank: 5,976,810 FCFA over the month.

[[piece:S-C-01-016]]

**The rule.** 585 debit, 5711 credit.

> **Why this choice and not another.** 585 avoids counting the same deposit twice.

**What it changes.** The till is back to zero.

**Where it is filed.** Stamped slips in the Cash binder. Journal CA.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Saturday 31 January: the bank statement

The statement shows the importer's payment, the installation customer's transfer, salaries and deposits. Patrice ticks it against 521.

[[piece:S-C-01-009]]

**The rule.** 401, 411, 422, 585, 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** A gap is explained before it is booked.

**What it changes.** 521 balance at January 31: 12,485,486 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement and reconciliation in the Bank binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

### January in brief

- Documents recorded, filed and reconciled.

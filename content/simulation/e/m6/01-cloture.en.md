### Thursday 31 December: counting the units

On 31 December Hervé counts the units left in the store and values them at the year's average cost.

[[piece:S-E-12-008]]

**The rule.** Under the cash basis the book follows money; counting the stock shows what the money spent has become.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why count what was not sold? Stock is tied-up cash: without it the year's surplus would be misread.

**What it changes.** WAC is the reference; FIFO is computed as a contrast.

**Where it is filed.** Signed stock card in the Payments binder; summary in the book.

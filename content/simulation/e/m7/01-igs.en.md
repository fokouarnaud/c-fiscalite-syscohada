### Monday 15 March 2027: the yearly IGS return

Hervé adds up the year's receipts: 18,960,000 FCFA. That turnover places the company in an IGS class: 500,000 FCFA due with the return, by 15 March 2027.

[[piece:S-E-15-001]]

**The rule.** Tariff by turnover class [[cgi:art. C 40]], yearly return [[cgi:art. C 41]].

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why no standard return? The IGS is a synthetic tax: a fixed amount by turnover class, halved for a member of a registered management centre (250,000 FCFA).

**What it changes.** The company stays below the 50 million threshold: it remains under the IGS.

**Where it is filed.** Return and receipt in the permanent file; a last line in the book.

**See again:** Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

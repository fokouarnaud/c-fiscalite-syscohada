### Friday 9 April 2027: the yearly IGS return

Hervé adds up the year's receipts: 18,960,000 FCFA. That turnover places the company in an IGS class: 500,000 FCFA due with the return, by 15 April 2027.

[[piece:S-E-16-001]]

**The rule.** Tariff by turnover class [[cgi:art. C 40]], yearly return [[cgi:art. C 41]].

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why a tariff and not a tax on profit? The IGS is a synthetic tax: a fixed amount by turnover class, halved for a member of a registered management centre (250,000 FCFA). The deadline is 15 April since the 2026 Finance Act, payment included; quarterly payment is an option.

**What it changes.** The company stays below the 50 million threshold: it remains under the IGS.

**Where it is filed.** Return and receipt in the permanent file; a last line in the book.

**See again:** Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Before 15 May 2027: the cash-basis tax return

With more than 10 million of turnover, the company keeps the cash-basis system and files a return by 15 May [[cgi:art. C 44]]: a balance sheet and an income statement drawn from cash, adjusted for the change in inventories. It is not the standard return.

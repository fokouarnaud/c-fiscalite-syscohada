### Thursday 5 February: the month's air conditioners

Hervé buys split air conditioners for resale. The supplier, under the actual regime, charges VAT and a 26,200 FCFA withholding because the company is under the IGS.

[[piece:S-E-02-001]]

**The rule.** 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: 5% because the company is under the IGS; VAT stays in the cost.

**What it changes.** A 651,070 FCFA payment.

**Where it is filed.** Payments binder; one line in the receipts and payments book.

**See again:** Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Tuesday 10 February: a job at École privée Les Palmiers

Hervé services École privée Les Palmiers's air conditioners; the invoice, without VAT, is paid by transfer.

[[piece:S-E-02-002]]

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: no VAT under the IGS.

**What it changes.** A 450,000 FCFA receipt, part of the IGS turnover.

**Where it is filed.** Receipts binder; one line in the receipts and payments book.

**See again:** Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Wednesday 25 February: training the customers

On Saturdays Mr Etoga trains customers' staff to use the units. As a civil servant he cannot be an employee: he issues a fee note, paid in cash.

[[piece:S-E-02-005]]

**The rule.** Fees 632, not wages.

> **Why this choice and not another.** An employment contract is in principle barred for a civil servant; a fee pays for a one-off service, with no payslip and no CNPS.

**What it changes.** A 60,000 FCFA payment.

**Where it is filed.** Payments binder; one line in the receipts and payments book.

**See again:** Accounting M10 “Other expenses and income”.

### Saturday 28 February: cash sales

Private customers buy units for cash: 750,000 FCFA in the month.

[[piece:S-E-02-004]]

**The rule.** Goods sold 7011, in cash 5711.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Under the cash basis the receipt is entered the day the money comes in, not the day of the order.

**What it changes.** The cash is deposited at the bank the next month.

**Where it is filed.** Receipts binder; one line in the receipts and payments book.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Saturday 28 February: the Orange Money statement

The Orange Money statement shows the customer's payment and the transfer to the bank, less 3,000 FCFA of fees.

[[piece:S-E-02-006]]

**The rule.** Sub-account 552 (5522), transfer through 585, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** A transfer between Orange Money and the bank is neither a receipt nor a payment: money only changes pockets.

**What it changes.** Only the fees are a payment in the book.

**Where it is filed.** Bank and Mobile Money binder, with the book ticked.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Saturday 28 February: the bank statement

The statement shows the Orange Money transfer, the cash deposit and the fees. Hervé ticks it line by line.

[[piece:S-E-02-007]]

**The rule.** Bank 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Ticking reveals anything missing from the book.

**What it changes.** The month's cash position is closed.

**Where it is filed.** Bank and Mobile Money binder, with the book ticked.

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

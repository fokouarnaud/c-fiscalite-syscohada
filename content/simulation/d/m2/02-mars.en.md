### Monday 2 March: the workshop rent

Jean-Claude pays Mrs Onana the workshop rent, less 15,000 FCFA withheld for tax.

[[piece:S-D-03-002]]

**The rule.** Gross rent 6222, withholding 4478.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Under the actual regime the tenant withholds and pays over: the expense remains the gross rent.

**What it changes.** The withholding joins the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Bank binder; lease in the permanent file. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Friday 6 March: restocking refrigerant and parts

Mrs Mvondo delivers the month's R410A cylinders and parts kits.

[[piece:S-D-03-004]]

**The rule.** Consumables 6041, VAT 4452, 2% withholding 4492.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: 6041 all year, stock counted on 31 December, withholding credited.

**What it changes.** Debt to the supplier: 3,831,500 FCFA, payable at the end of next month.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Purchases binder with the delivery note. Journal AC.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”.

### Sunday 15 March: the 15th return

Jean-Claude files February's VAT, instalment and withholdings and pays 38,246 FCFA.

[[piece:S-D-03-009]]

**The rule.** VAT due on collection less recoverable VAT, withheld VAT included; 2% instalment less withholdings.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why is the net instalment often nil? Public withholdings exceed it; the excess waits.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by 38,246 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Return and acknowledgement in the Tax returns binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audit”.

### Friday 20 March: the public customer's payment

The Treasury pays the invoice of Hôpital de district de Biyem-Assi (fictif): out of 2,862,000 FCFA it withholds 132,000 FCFA of tax instalment and 462,000 FCFA of VAT, and transfers 2,268,000 FCFA. Mr Abena hands over the withholding certificate.

[[piece:S-D-03-003]]

**The rule.** 5% withholding plus council cents in 4492; VAT withheld at source, deductible with the certificate (4458); VAT becomes due: 471 cleared to 4432.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why not book the net as revenue? The customer paid the whole invoice: part to Jean-Claude, part to the tax office on his behalf. Both withholdings are advances on his own taxes, not discounts.

**What it changes.** The public receivable is cleared; two tax advances await the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Payment notice and certificate in the Tax returns binder: the certificate supports the deduction. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M4 “VAT and excise duties”; Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Tuesday 24 March: repairs for businesses

Jean-Claude and Brice made 50 call-outs at Résidence hôtelière Les Manguiers (fictive). The invoice goes with the signed job sheets, payable on the 10th of next month.

[[piece:S-D-03-005]]

**The rule.** Services 706, customer 411; VAT pending 471, due on collection.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: 471 on invoicing, 4432 on payment.

**What it changes.** A receivable on the customer.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice copy in the Sales binder with the signed job sheets. Journal VT.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Taxation M4 “VAT and excise duties”.

### Thursday 26 March: the public maintenance contract

This month 100 units serviced for Commune d'arrondissement de Yaoundé VI (fictive). The contract stays under 5 million before tax.

[[piece:S-D-03-006]]

**The rule.** Same entry as for a private customer: 411, 706, 471.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: withholdings are booked on payment, with the certificate.

**What it changes.** A receivable on the public payer, paid next month.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice copy in the Sales binder with the signed job sheets. Journal VT.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Tuesday 31 March: home repairs

Households paid 1,335,600 FCFA by Orange Money; the operator takes its fees and the balance goes to the bank.

[[piece:S-D-03-007]]

**The rule.** 552, 706; invoice and payment the same day: 471 cleared to 4432 at once; fees 631, transfer through 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why go through 471 if payment is immediate? The software applies one rule to all services: VAT waits in 471 and falls due on payment, here the same day.

**What it changes.** VAT on these repairs is due this month.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Journal OM.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”; Taxation M4 “VAT and excise duties”.

### Tuesday 31 March: Brice's pay

Jean-Claude prepares Brice's payslip and transfers 115,464 FCFA net.

[[piece:S-D-03-008]]

**The rule.** Gross 6611, levies, withholdings, net 422.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Brice is an employee; Jean-Claude has no payslip: one does not employ oneself.

**What it changes.** Withholdings join the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Signed payslip in the Payroll binder. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M11 “Payroll”.

### Tuesday 31 March: the bank statement

The statement shows the private customer's transfer, the supplier's payment, the wage, the Orange Money transfer and the 300,000 FCFA Jean-Claude moves to his personal account for his household.

[[piece:S-D-03-001]]

**The rule.** 411 and VAT due, 401, 422, 585, drawings 1042, 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** A gap is explained before it is booked.

**What it changes.** 521 balance at March 31: 6,670,241 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement and reconciliation in the Bank binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

### Thursday 31 December: the count

On the 31st Jean-Claude and Brice count the refrigerant cylinders and parts kits left in the workshop and value them at the year's average cost.

[[piece:S-D-12-010]]

**The rule.** Closing stock in 331, through 6033.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why 6033 and not 6031? These are not goods for resale but supplies used up; their change has its own account.

**What it changes.** Closing stock: 3,294,000 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Count sheets in the Inventory binder. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M20 “Inventories at year end”.

### Thursday 31 December: depreciating the van

Jean-Claude depreciates the van over four years, from January.

[[piece:S-D-12-011]]

**The rule.** Charge 6813, depreciation 2845; at most 25% for light vehicles.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why depreciate a contributed asset that cost the workshop nothing? It wears out in the workshop's service; its contribution value is its cost.

**What it changes.** 1,500,000 FCFA of expense.

**Where it is filed.** Asset register. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M18 “Depreciation”.

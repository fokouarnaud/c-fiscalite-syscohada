### Monday 3 August: the workshop rent

Jean-Claude pays Mrs Onana the workshop rent, less 15,000 FCFA withheld for tax.

[[piece:S-D-08-002]]

**The rule.** Gross rent 6222, withholding 4478.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Under the actual regime the tenant withholds and pays over: the expense remains the gross rent.

**What it changes.** The withholding joins the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Bank binder; lease in the permanent file. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Saturday 15 August: the 15th return

Jean-Claude files July's VAT, instalment and withholdings and pays 38,246 FCFA.

[[piece:S-D-08-009]]

**The rule.** VAT due on collection less recoverable VAT, withheld VAT included; 2% instalment less withholdings.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Regularity avoids penalties.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by 38,246 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Return and acknowledgement in the Tax returns binder. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audit”.

### Thursday 20 August: the public customer's payment

The Treasury pays the invoice of Commune d'arrondissement de Yaoundé VI (fictive): out of 2,146,500 FCFA it withholds 99,000 FCFA of tax instalment and 346,500 FCFA of VAT, and transfers 1,701,000 FCFA. Mr Abena hands over the withholding certificate.

[[piece:S-D-08-003]]

**The rule.** 5% withholding plus council cents in 4492; VAT withheld at source, deductible with the certificate (4458); VAT becomes due: 471 cleared to 4432.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why not book the net as revenue? The customer paid the whole invoice: part to Jean-Claude, part to the tax office on his behalf. Both withholdings are advances on his own taxes, not discounts.

**What it changes.** The public receivable is cleared; two tax advances await the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Payment notice and certificate in the Tax returns binder: the certificate supports the deduction. Journal BQ.

**See again:** Taxation M4 “VAT and excise duties”; Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Monday 31 August: home repairs

Households paid 751,275 FCFA by Orange Money; the operator takes its fees and the balance goes to the bank.

[[piece:S-D-08-007]]

**The rule.** 552, 706; invoice and payment the same day: 471 cleared to 4432 at once; fees 631, transfer through 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why go through 471 if payment is immediate? The software applies one rule to all services: VAT waits in 471 and falls due on payment, here the same day.

**What it changes.** VAT on these repairs is due this month.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Journal OM.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”; Taxation M4 “VAT and excise duties”.

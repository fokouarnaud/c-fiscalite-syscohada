## Ets Bilounga Boissons

Drinks depot in Yaoundé-Ekounou, the sole proprietorship of Mrs Thérèse Bilounga, registered on 2 January 2026. Forecast turnover of 28 million, below the 50 million trading threshold: synthetic general tax (IGS) [[cgi:art. 93 quater]] [[cgi:art. C 39]], local tax office. Above 10 million of turnover the depot keeps the cash-basis system and files a cash-basis return [[cgi:art. C 44]] [[source:ohada-audcif]].

### Tools and routines

Thérèse keeps a receipts and payments book [[source:ohada-audcif]], a daily cash book and a stock card. Every evening: till count. On the 15th and at month end: cash deposit.

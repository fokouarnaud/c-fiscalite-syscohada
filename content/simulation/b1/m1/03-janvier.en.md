January 2026: Thérèse Bilounga opens her depot in Ekounou. Read each scene, then answer the quiz [[cgi:art. C 38]].

### Friday 2 January: Thérèse's savings

Thérèse pays 3,000,000 FCFA of savings into the depot account: enough for the brewery's first two deliveries.

[[piece:S-B1-01-005]]

**The rule.** Personal capital 103, bank 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why no share capital? The depot is not a company: Thérèse trades in her own name.

**What it changes.** Starting cash is in the bank; no receipt.

**Where it is filed.** Permanent file.

**See again:** Accounting M3 “Assets and liabilities”.

### Monday 5 January: the first delivery

Mr Fouda delivers 130 crates of 65 cl at 6,100 FCFA before tax. Thérèse pays 985,303 FCFA by transfer, VAT and withholding included.

[[piece:S-B1-01-006]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011 at full cost; 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why all in the cost? Under the IGS Thérèse charges no VAT, so recovers none; and the CGI credits the withholding only against income taxes, which the IGS replaces.

**What it changes.** A 985,303 FCFA payment; the stock card gains 130 crates.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Payments binder with the signed delivery note; a line in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Thursday 15 January: the rent

Thérèse hands Mr Owona 50,000 FCFA in cash, out of the fortnight's takings.

[[piece:S-B1-01-010]]

**The rule.** Rent 6222 paid gross.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why no 10% withholding? It applies to tenants under the actual regime; an IGS tenant pays gross.

**What it changes.** A cash payment in the book.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Payments binder.

**See again:** Accounting M10 “Other expenses and income”.

### Saturday 31 January: cash sales

At the counter Thérèse and Junior sold 154 crates for cash, full against empty: 1,293,600 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B1-01-008]]

**The rule.** Receipts 7011 in the till 5711, no VAT.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why no deposit? The customer returns an empty crate for a full one: nothing to refund.

**What it changes.** Receipts grow, and the till, until the deposit.

**Where it is filed.** Cash book in the Receipts binder.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

### Saturday 31 January: Orange Money payments

One customer in three pays by phone: 638,400 FCFA this month; the operator takes its fees and the balance goes to the bank.

[[piece:S-B1-01-009]]

**The rule.** 552, fees 631, 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why 585? It avoids counting the same money twice.

**What it changes.** Orange Money receipts reach the bank.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Saturday 31 January: the push-carts

The push-cart men delivered customers: 23 trips at 1,500 F.

[[piece:S-B1-01-011]]

**The rule.** Transport on sales 612, paid in cash.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why 612 and not wages? They are not employees: paid by the trip.

**What it changes.** A transport payment in the book.

**Where it is filed.** Push-cart slips in the Payments binder.

**See again:** Accounting M10 “Other expenses and income”.

### Saturday 31 January: the bank deposits

On the 15th and the last day Thérèse banks the takings: 576,000 FCFA then 613,100 FCFA. The till keeps 20,000 of float.

[[piece:S-B1-01-012]]

**The rule.** 585 debit, 5711 credit.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why not keep it all in the till? Banking limits risk and leaves a trace.

**What it changes.** The till is back to its 20,000 float.

**Where it is filed.** Stamped slips in the Bank binder.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Saturday 31 January: the bank statement

The statement shows deposits, the Orange Money transfer, the brewery transfers and the 100,000 Thérèse moves to her personal account.

[[piece:S-B1-01-013]]

**The rule.** 585, drawings 1042, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why is the personal transfer not a payment of the business? It is drawings: it reduces equity, not profit.

**What it changes.** 521 balance at January 31: 2,750,394 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement and reconciliation in the Bank binder.

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

### January in brief

- Receipts and payments booked, documents filed, bank and Orange Money reconciled.
- No monthly return: under the IGS everything is filed once a year.

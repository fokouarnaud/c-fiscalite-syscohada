On 31 December the year closes with the count [[syscohada:5711]].

### Thursday 31 December: the crate count

On the evening of the 31st Thérèse and Junior count the full crates: the 30 bought ahead in January are still there, valued at the year's average cost.

[[piece:S-B1-12-009]]

**The rule.** Under the cash basis there is no inventory entry; closing stock adjusts the cash-basis return.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why WAC on brewery HT prices? It is the stock card reference; FIFO keeps the latest, dearer crates.

**What it changes.** Closing stock: 183,930 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Signed stock card; summary in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M20 “Inventories at year end”.

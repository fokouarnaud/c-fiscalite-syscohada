### Thursday 5 November: the first delivery

Mr Fouda delivers 140 crates of 65 cl at 6,160 FCFA before tax. Thérèse pays 1,071,532 FCFA by transfer, VAT and withholding included.

[[piece:S-B1-11-001]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011 at full cost; 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: VAT and withholding stay in the cost.

**What it changes.** A 1,071,532 FCFA payment; the stock card gains 140 crates.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Payments binder with the signed delivery note; a line in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Monday 30 November: cash sales

At the counter Thérèse and Junior sold 187 crates for cash, full against empty: 1,570,800 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B1-11-003]]

**The rule.** Receipts 7011 in the till 5711, no VAT.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The evening cash book backs the book.

**What it changes.** Receipts grow, and the till, until the deposit.

**Where it is filed.** Cash book in the Receipts binder.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

### Monday 30 November: Orange Money payments

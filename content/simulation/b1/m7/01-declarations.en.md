Three deadlines in 2027 [[cgi:art. C 41]].

### Wednesday 10 March 2027: the income return

Before 15 March Thérèse files her yearly income return: 26,292,000 FCFA of turnover, under the IGS.

[[piece:S-B1-15-001]]

**The rule.** An individual under the IGS still files the art. 74 yearly return.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why file if the IGS discharges income tax? The tax office cross-checks each person's income; no extra tax on the depot.

**What it changes.** No payment at this date.

**Where it is filed.** Acknowledgement in the permanent file.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Friday 9 April 2027: the year's IGS

Turnover places the depot in class 9: 1,000,000 FCFA due with the return by 15 April. As a management-centre member she would have paid 500,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B1-16-001]]

**The rule.** Tariff by class; return and payment by 15 April, or quarterly by option.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why is the IGS so heavy? It ignores the margin: on a 1,250,488 FCFA profit, a 1,000,000 FCFA IGS takes a large share.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by 1,000,000 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Return and receipt in the permanent file.

**See again:** Taxation M6 “Local taxation”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Before 15 May 2027: the cash-basis return

With more than 10 million of turnover, Thérèse files the cash-basis return by 15 May [[cgi:art. C 44]]: a balance sheet and an income statement drawn from cash, adjusted for closing stock.

[[piece:S-B1-17-002]]

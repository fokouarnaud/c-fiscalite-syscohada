### Monday 2 March: the shop rent

Alain pays Mr Tchoua the Akwa shop rent, less the 30,000 FCFA withholding.

[[piece:S-A-03-002]]

**The rule.** Gross rent 6222, withholding 4478.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The expense is the gross rent; the withholding is a debt to the State.

**What it changes.** The withholding joins the next 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Bank and Mobile Money binder; lease in the permanent file. Bank journal (BQ).

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Thursday 5 March: restocking at the wholesaler

Mr Din delivers 120 shirts, 80 trousers and 60 dresses; Brenda counts the boxes and signs the delivery note. Sawa Textiles is paid by transfer on the 12th.

[[piece:S-A-03-003]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011, VAT 4452, 2% withholding 4492, supplier 401.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: the withholding is an advance on tax; stock is counted only on 31 December.

**What it changes.** Sawa Textiles receives 4,134,625 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Purchases binder, with the delivery note signed by Brenda. Purchases journal (AC).

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”.

### Tuesday 10 March: a formal notice

The owner of the next building claims 500,000 FCFA for a wall damaged while the shop was being fitted out. Clarisse disputes it, but the risk of paying is real.

[[piece:S-A-03-009]]

**The rule.** Litigation provision: charge 6911, provision 191.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Nothing is paid yet, but prudence requires booking the risk once it is probable. Tax law does not accept it here: the charge will be added back.

**What it changes.** Accounting profit drops; taxable profit does not.

**Where it is filed.** Document in its binder, with a note for the tax bridge.

**See again:** Accounting M22 “Provisions for risks and charges”.

### Sunday 15 March: the 15th return

Alain files February's VAT, CIT instalment and withholdings online, and pays 1,063,871 FCFA.

[[piece:S-A-03-004]]

**The rule.** VAT charged less VAT recoverable; 2% instalment plus council cents, less withholding suffered; withholdings paid over.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The withholding paid to Sawa Textiles reduces the instalment: it is not lost.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by 1,063,871 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Return and acknowledgement in the Tax returns binder. Bank journal (BQ).

**See again:** Accounting M5 “VAT: mechanism and return”; Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audit”.

### Tuesday 31 March: the takings go to the bank

Every week Brenda takes the cash takings to the bank; on the last day of the month Alain gathers the stamped slips: 4,770,000 FCFA deposited.

[[piece:S-A-03-010]]

**The rule.** 585 debit, till 5711 credit; the bank clears 585 on receipt.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The internal transfer goes through 585 so that one document carries each entry.

**What it changes.** The till empties, the bank fills; profit does not move.

**Where it is filed.** Stamped slip in the Cash binder. Cash journal (CA).

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Tuesday 31 March: counter sales

Akwa customers pay cash: 4,770,000 FCFA over the month, counted every evening in the cash book.

[[piece:S-A-03-005]]

**The rule.** Cash 5711 incl. tax, sales 7011, VAT 4431.

> **Why this choice and not another.** VAT collected does not belong to the shop: it is paid over on the 15th.

**What it changes.** Turnover rises, and with it the CIT instalment.

**Where it is filed.** Daily cash book pages and summary in the Cash binder. Cash journal (CA).

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Tuesday 31 March: the Orange Money statement

The month's Orange Money statement: 2,385,000 FCFA paid by phone, less 9,500 FCFA of operator fees.

[[piece:S-A-03-006]]

**The rule.** Sub-account 552 (5522), sales 7011, VAT 4431, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** One sub-account per operator: each is reconciled with its own statement. Fees are a service, not a discount to customers.

**What it changes.** The balance moves to the bank at month end, via 585.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Orange Money journal (OM).

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Tuesday 31 March: payroll

Alain prepares the payslips for the manager and the two assistants and transfers 610,966 FCFA of net pay.

[[piece:S-A-03-008]]

**The rule.** Gross 6611, employer levies 6413 and 6641, withholdings 4472 and 431, net 422.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Withholdings are debts to the State and the CNPS, not expenses.

**What it changes.** Withholdings join the 15th return.

**Where it is filed.** Signed payslips in the Payroll binder. Miscellaneous journal (OD).

**See again:** Accounting M11 “Payroll”.

### Tuesday 31 March: the statement and reconciliation

Bank X's statement shows the Sawa Textiles payment, salaries, internal transfers and fees. Alain ticks it against 521.

[[piece:S-A-03-001]]

**The rule.** Supplier 401, salaries 422, transfers 585, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** A gap is explained before it is booked.

**What it changes.** 521 balance at March 31: 14,593,759 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement and signed reconciliation in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Bank journal (BQ).

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

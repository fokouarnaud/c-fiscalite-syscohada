**Tools.** Alain keeps the books in accounting software (Sage 100 Comptabilité, Odoo or any AUDCIF-compliant software); it validates each entry and closes periods [[source:ohada-audcif]].

| Support | Contents | Kept by |

| Purchases, Sales, Bank and Mobile Money, Cash, Payroll, Fixed-asset, Tax returns binders | The year's documents, in order, marked with journal code and entry number | Alain |

Software journals: purchases (AC), sales (VT), bank (BQ), cash (CA), MTN MoMo (MM), Orange Money (OM), miscellaneous (OD).

**Routines.** Every evening: till count and the day's Mobile Money payments. Every week: record and file the documents. Every month: bank and Mobile Money reconciliations, trial balance, 15th return. Every quarter: close the periods. Every year: count the three families, inventory book, tax return; everything is kept ten years.

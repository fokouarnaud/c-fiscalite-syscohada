### Thursday 31 December: counting the three families

On the evening of the 31st Brenda and Sandrine count shirts, trousers and dresses, shelf by shelf; Alain values each family at the year's weighted average cost.

[[piece:S-A-12-010]]

**The rule.** Closing stock in 3111, 3112 and 3121, through 6031.

> **Why this choice and not another.** WAC smooths the wholesaler's price rises; FIFO would value stock at the latest, higher prices.

**What it changes.** Closing stock of 1,854,375 FCFA reduces cost of sales.

**Where it is filed.** Count sheets and WAC cards in the Inventory binder; summary in the inventory book. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M20 “Inventories at year end”.

### Thursday 31 December: depreciating the car

The car has run since April: nine months over three years, 3,000,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-A-12-011]]

**The rule.** Charge 6813, accumulated depreciation 2845.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The book rate (33.33%) exceeds the 25% maximum tax rate for a light car in town: the gap is added back.

**What it changes.** 3,000,000 FCFA of expense, no cash out.

**Where it is filed.** Asset register in the Fixed-asset binder. Journal OD.

**See again:** Accounting M18 “Depreciation”.

### Saturday 10 April 2027: the year's tax

At the 25% reduced rate CIT reaches 9,788,350 FCFA, above the minimum tax (1,980,000 FCFA): CIT is due. Instalments paid are deducted.

[[piece:S-A-16-001]]

**The rule.** CIT due = higher of CIT and the minimum; tax 891, State 441.

> **Why this choice and not another.** A high-margin shop pays CIT on its profit; the minimum only bites when profit is low: compare with the beverage depot of track B3.

**What it changes.** Balance due with the return: 7,808,350 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Computation attached to the return, in the Tax returns binder. Journal OD, at 31 December.

**See again:** Taxation M2 “Corporate income tax”.

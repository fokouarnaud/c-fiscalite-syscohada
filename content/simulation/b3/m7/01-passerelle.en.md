### Saturday 10 April 2027: the year's tax

With a taxable profit of 2,044,790 FCFA, CIT at 25% would be only 562,100 FCFA, council cents included. But the depot sold 286,098,000 FCFA: the minimum tax, 6,294,156 FCFA, prevails. The instalments paid every 15th already cover it.

[[piece:S-B3-16-001]]

**The rule.** CIT due is the higher of computed CIT and the minimum tax; tax 891 against State 441.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why does a beverage depot pay the minimum? Its margin is thin, a few hundred francs a crate. The minimum is computed on turnover, not on profit: that is the reality of distribution.

**What it changes.** Net loss: 4,403,876 FCFA. The depot made money before tax, but the minimum tax, computed on turnover, exceeds that profit.

**Where it is filed.** CIT computation attached to the return, in the Tax returns binder. Entry in the miscellaneous journal (OD), dated 31 December 2026.

**See again:** Taxation M2 “Corporate income tax”.

August 2026: an expense paid without an invoice. The month opens with the trial balance closed the day before, taken from the official ledger [[syscohada:521]].

### Monday 3 August: the rent

As every month, Serge pays Mrs Owona the warehouse rent, less the 25,000 FCFA withholding.

[[piece:S-B3-08-002]]

**The rule.** Gross rent 6222, withholding 4478.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder from January: the expense is the gross rent; the withholding belongs to the State.

**What it changes.** The withholding goes into the 15th September return.

**Where it is filed.** Receipt in the Bank and Mobile Money binder with the transfer order; the original lease goes to the permanent file. Entry in the bank journal (BQ); Serge notes the withholding for the 15th return.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Wednesday 5 August: the brewery invoice

Mr Fouda sends the August invoice: 3,050 crates of 65 cl and 520 of 33 cl, delivered in several runs. On the same invoice the brewery refunds the deposit on the empties Didier returned, 13,200,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B3-08-003]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011, VAT 4452, withholding 4492; deposit on full crates debited to 4094, refund on empties credited.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The refund is neither income nor a purchase discount: it clears the 4094 receivable created when the crates arrived full.

**What it changes.** The depot owes the brewery 27,823,030 FCFA; at August 31, 15,408,000 FCFA of deposits are still to be recovered.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Purchases binder, stapled to the delivery notes signed by Didier; the same notes feed the stock card every evening. Entry in the purchases journal (AC) on the day the invoice arrives.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”.

### Saturday 15 August: the 15th return

Serge files July's taxes online and pays them the same day: VAT, CIT instalment and withholdings, 225,643 FCFA in all.

[[piece:S-B3-08-008]]

**The rule.** VAT charged less VAT recoverable; CIT instalment of 2% of turnover plus council cents, less the withholding suffered; rent and payroll withholdings paid over.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Nothing new in the method: regularity matters, a late return costs penalties.

**What it changes.** The bank drops by 225,643 FCFA; VAT credit carried forward: 1 964 311 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Return, acknowledgement and payment notice in the Tax returns binder. Entry in the bank journal (BQ).

**See again:** Accounting M5 “VAT: mechanism and return”; Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audit”.

### Wednesday 19 August: an expense without an invoice

A flat tyre on the Mbalmayo road: a roadside mechanic fixes the truck, paid in cash, with no invoice. Serge writes an internal voucher.

[[piece:S-B3-08-009]]

**The rule.** Sundry expenses 6588, cash 5711; an expense without a supporting document is not deductible.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The expense is real and is recorded; but an internal voucher proves nothing to the tax office: it will be added back. Good practice is to ask for a receipt, even handwritten.

**What it changes.** 60,000 FCFA leave the till.

**Where it is filed.** Internal voucher in the Cash binder, signed by Serge, with a note for the tax bridge. Entry in the cash journal (CA).

**See again:** Taxation M2 “Corporate income tax”.

### Thursday 20 August: the Rond-Point order

Mrs Ngah orders 120 crates of 65 cl and 30 of 33 cl for her snack bar; she returns last month's empties, refunded on the invoice.

[[piece:S-B3-08-004]]

**The rule.** Sales 7011, VAT 4431, customer 411; deposit in 4194, outside the VAT base.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The refund settles a debt: it is debited to 4194, never deducted from turnover.

**What it changes.** Mrs Ngah owes 1,234,953 FCFA, payable on the 10th of next month.

**Where it is filed.** Copy of the invoice in the Sales binder, in number order with no gaps; Didier enters the deposited crates in the empties book under Mrs Ngah. Entry in the sales journal (VT).

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Monday 31 August: the month's till

After a month of daily till counts, Didier totals the August cash book, 17,057,520 FCFA taken at the counter.

[[piece:S-B3-08-005]]

**The rule.** Cash 5711 incl. tax, sales 7011, VAT 4431.

> **Why this choice and not another.** One monthly summary entry is lawful, provided the daily cash book supports it.

**What it changes.** The month's VAT collected rises accordingly.

**Where it is filed.** Every evening Didier counts the cash and enters it in the cash book, signed with Serge; the monthly summary goes to the Cash binder. Entry in the cash journal (CA).

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”.

### Monday 31 August: the Orange Money statement

The August statement arrives: bar payments, operator fees, transfer to the bank.

[[piece:S-B3-08-006]]

**The rule.** 552 (5522), fees 631, transfer through 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Fees stay bank services (631), never a sales discount.

**What it changes.** 10,128,227 FCFA leave for the bank.

**Where it is filed.** Statement downloaded from the merchant area, printed and filed in the Bank and Mobile Money binder. Entry in the Orange Money journal (OM).

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Monday 31 August: payroll

Serge prints the three August payslips and transfers the net pay.

[[piece:S-B3-08-007]]

**The rule.** Gross 6611, employer levies 6413 and 6641; withholdings 4472 and 431, net 422.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: withholdings are debts to the State and the CNPS, not expenses.

**What it changes.** 595,013 FCFA paid to staff.

**Where it is filed.** Signed payslips in the Payroll binder, a copy handed to each employee. Entry in the miscellaneous journal (OD).

**See again:** Accounting M11 “Payroll”.

### Monday 31 August: the weekly deposits

Serge gathers the August deposit slips: 16,997,520 FCFA taken to the bank.

[[piece:S-B3-08-010]]

**The rule.** 585 debit, 5711 credit.

> **Why this choice and not another.** 585 keeps one entry per document: the till pays out, the bank receives.

**What it changes.** The till is back to zero at month end.

**Where it is filed.** Slips stamped by the bank, stapled to the cash book in the Cash binder. Entry in the cash journal (CA).

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Monday 31 August: the statement and reconciliation

Bank X's statement arrives: brewery payment, Mrs Ngah's transfer, salaries, deposits, loan instalment. Serge ticks each line against 521.

[[piece:S-B3-08-001]]

**The rule.** Brewery 401, customer 411, salaries 422, internal transfers 585, loan 162 and interest 6712, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The instalment splits in two: principal reduces the debt (162), only interest is an expense (6712).

**What it changes.** Book balance of 521 at August 31: 8,271,445 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder, with the reconciliation signed by Serge. Entries in the bank journal (BQ), then reconciliation in the software before closing the month.

**See again:** Accounting M15 “Financing”.

### The depot's tools and routines

**Tools.** Serge keeps the books in accounting software (Sage 100 Comptabilité, Odoo or any software that complies with the AUDCIF); a paper journal would be lawful too. The software must validate each entry and close periods irreversibly [[source:ohada-audcif]].

| Support | Contents | Kept by |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent file | Articles, notarised declaration, capital certificate, lease, trade register, taxpayer card | Serge |
| Purchases, Sales, Bank and Mobile Money, Cash, Payroll, Fixed-asset binders | The year's documents, in date order, each marked with its journal code and entry number | Serge |
| Cash book (notebook) | The day's cash in and out, signed every evening | Didier |
| Stock card | Full crates in and out, by format | Didier |
| Empties book | Crates on deposit with each customer and those returned | Didier |

Software journals: purchases (AC), sales (VT), bank (BQ), cash (CA), Orange Money (OM), miscellaneous (OD).

**Routines.**

- *Every evening*: Didier closes the till, counts the empties and updates the stock card; he and Serge sign the cash book.
- *Every week*: takings go to the bank; Serge records and files the week's documents.
- *Every month*: bank and Orange Money reconciliations, trial balance, journal centralisation, 15th return.
- *Every quarter*: periods are closed in the software.
- *Every year*: physical count, inventory book, financial statements and tax return; books and documents are kept for ten years.

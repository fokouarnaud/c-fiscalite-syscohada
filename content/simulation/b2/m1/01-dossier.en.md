## Ets Essomba Boissons

Drinks depot in Douala-Bépanda, the sole proprietorship of Mr Paul Essomba, registered on 2 January 2026. Forecast turnover of 46 million, below the 50 million trading threshold: synthetic general tax (IGS) [[cgi:art. 93 quater]] [[cgi:art. C 39]], Douala V local tax office. Above 10 million of turnover the depot keeps the cash-basis system and files a cash-basis return [[cgi:art. C 44]] [[source:ohada-audcif]].

This track has a twist: the depot grows. Crossing 50 million during the year would mean a forced move to the actual regime [[cgi:art. C 39]]; Paul stays below the threshold in 2026, then, when a hotel orders its drinks for 2027, turns his business into **Essomba Boissons SARL**, under the actual regime, on 1 January 2027.

### Tools and routines

Paul keeps a receipts and payments book [[source:ohada-audcif]], a daily cash book and a stock card. Every evening: till count. On the 15th and at month end: cash deposit. Every month: reconciliations and a check of cumulative turnover against the 50 million threshold. Every year: crate count; income return by 15 March; at cessation, a closing return within three months [[cgi:art. 95]]; documents are kept ten years.

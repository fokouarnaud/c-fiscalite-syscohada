Two deadlines for the business in 2027, the second brought forward by cessation [[cgi:art. 95]].

### Wednesday 10 March 2027: the income return

Before 15 March Paul files his yearly income return: 46,872,000 FCFA of turnover, under the IGS.

[[piece:S-B2-15-001]]

**The rule.** An individual under the IGS still files the art. 74 yearly return.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why file if the IGS discharges income tax? The tax office cross-checks each person's income.

**What it changes.** No payment at this date.

**Where it is filed.** Acknowledgement in the permanent file.

**See again:** Taxation M3 “Personal income tax”.

### Friday 26 March 2027: the closing return

Within three months of cessation Paul files the closing return: turnover, stock transferred, transferee. He pays the class 10 IGS with it, 2,000,000 FCFA, out of the balance kept on 31 December.

[[piece:S-B2-15-002]]

**The rule.** Closing return within three months, with payment; tariff by class.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why pay in March, not by 15 April? Cessation brings the deadline forward. As a management-centre member he would have paid 1,000,000 FCFA.

**What it changes.** The business has no obligation left; the LLC follows the actual regime at the CIME.

**Where it is filed.** Return and receipt in the business's permanent file, kept ten years.

**See again:** Taxation M7 “Filing obligations and audits”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### With the closing return: the cash-basis return

With more than 10 million of turnover, the business files the cash-basis return [[cgi:art. C 44]]: a balance sheet and an income statement drawn from cash, adjusted for closing stock. Paul files it with the closing return.

### And the LLC?

From February 2027, Essomba Boissons SARL files monthly returns at the Douala CIME: VAT on the hotel's purchases, deductible VAT (the notary's included), tax instalment, 2% withholding on its purchases [[cgi:art. 21]]. Track B3, under the actual regime, shows the detail.

### Thursday 5 November: the first delivery

The brewery truck delivers 240 crates of 65 cl at 6,160 FCFA before tax. Paul pays 1,836,912 FCFA by transfer, VAT and withholding included.

[[piece:S-B2-11-002]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011 at full cost; 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: VAT and withholding stay in the cost.

**What it changes.** A 1,836,912 FCFA payment; the stock card gains 240 crates.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Payments binder with the signed delivery note; a line in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Monday 16 November: the LLC's cash

Paul transfers 3,182,200 FCFA from the depot account to a blocked account in the name of Essomba Boissons SARL, being formed. The bank hands him the depositary's certificate.

[[piece:S-B2-11-004]]

**The rule.** Funds deposited on subscription, blocked until registration; for the depot, drawings 1042.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why drawings and not a payment? The depot's money is Paul's: he takes it back to contribute it, in his own name, to the company, a separate person born at registration.

**What it changes.** The depot's bank falls; the LLC's capital is on its way.

**Where it is filed.** "LLC" binder: it goes to the LLC with its founding documents.

**See again:** Accounting M3 “Assets and liabilities”; Accounting M15 “Financing operations”.

### Monday 23 November: the tricycle

To deliver the hotel in 2027 Paul buys a box tricycle at Moto Plus: 1,450,080 FCFA VAT included, in his own name, paid from the depot account.

[[piece:S-B2-11-005]]

**The rule.** Personal purchase: drawings 1042; it will enter the LLC as a contribution in kind (2458).

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why not buy it in the depot's name? The cash-basis depot does not track fixed assets and ceases in a month; bought by Paul then contributed, the tricycle enters the LLC at a value set in the articles. The VAT paid is not recoverable.

**What it changes.** The depot's bank falls again; Paul holds an asset he will contribute.

**Where it is filed.** "LLC" binder: it goes to the LLC with its founding documents.

**See again:** Accounting M13 “Fixed assets”.

### Monday 30 November: cash sales

At the counter Paul and Brigitte sold 320 crates for cash, full against empty: 2,688,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B2-11-006]]

**The rule.** Receipts 7011 in the till 5711, no VAT.

> **Why this choice and not another.** The evening cash book backs the book.

**What it changes.** Receipts grow, and the till, until the deposit.

**Where it is filed.** Cash book in the Receipts binder.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

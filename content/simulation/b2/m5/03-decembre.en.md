### Saturday 5 December: the first delivery

The brewery truck delivers 260 crates of 65 cl at 6,160 FCFA before tax. Paul pays 1,989,988 FCFA by transfer, VAT and withholding included.

[[piece:S-B2-12-004]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011 at full cost; 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: VAT and withholding stay in the cost.

**What it changes.** A 1,989,988 FCFA payment; the stock card gains 260 crates.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Payments binder with the signed delivery note; a line in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Monday 28 December: the articles

At Maître Ngo Bassa's office, Paul signs the articles of Essomba Boissons SARL: he contributes the crates he will hold on 31 December, at the year's average cost, his tricycle and the cash already deposited. Capital: 5,000,000 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B2-12-006]]

**The rule.** Each contribution in kind is valued; an auditor is needed above 5 million; a notarised deed above one million of capital; on 1 January 2027, capital 1013 against stock 311, tricycle 2458 and bank 521.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why a single-member LLC? Paul stays sole master of his business, but his personal assets are now ring-fenced: he risks only his contributions.

**What it changes.** No entry for the depot: the contribution takes effect on 1 January, when the LLC opens its balance sheet.

**Where it is filed.** "LLC" binder: it goes to the LLC with its founding documents.

**See again:** Accounting M3 “Assets and liabilities”; Accounting M25 “Closing, reopening and appropriation”.

### Monday 28 December: the notary's invoice

The notary bills fees and the registration duty he pays over to the Treasury: 398,125 FCFA, advanced by Paul from the depot account.

[[piece:S-B2-12-007]]

**The rule.** 2% degressive duty on the capital; for the depot, drawings; on 1 January the LLC takes over the invoice: fees 6324, duty 6468, recoverable VAT 4454, debt to Paul 4621.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why expenses and not set-up costs? Revised SYSCOHADA no longer capitalises formation costs: they are the LLC's expenses, by nature.

**What it changes.** The LLC starts 2027 owing its partner and with VAT to recover.

**Where it is filed.** "LLC" binder: it goes to the LLC with its founding documents.

**See again:** Taxation M5 “Registration and stamp duty”; Accounting M4 “Expenses and income”.

### Thursday 31 December: cash sales

At the counter Paul and Brigitte sold 347 crates for cash, full against empty: 2,914,800 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B2-12-008]]

**The rule.** Receipts 7011 in the till 5711, no VAT.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why track the running total? Above 50 million in the year, the depot would be moved to the actual regime.

**What it changes.** Receipts grow, and the till, until the deposit.

**Where it is filed.** Cash book in the Receipts binder.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

### Thursday 31 December: Orange Money payments

### Thursday 31 December: the bank deposits

On the 15th and the last day Paul banks the takings: 1,378,000 FCFA then 1,403,800 FCFA. On 31 December the till is emptied: the depot ceases.

[[piece:S-B2-12-012]]

**The rule.** 585 debit, 5711 credit.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why empty the till? The business ceases; the LLC will have its own.

**What it changes.** The till is at zero.

**Where it is filed.** Stamped slips in the Bank binder.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

### Thursday 31 December: the bank statement

The statement shows deposits, the Orange Money transfer, the brewery transfers and the 120,000 Paul moves to his personal account. On the 31st Paul moves the balance to his personal account and closes the depot account.

[[piece:S-B2-12-013]]

**The rule.** 585, drawings 1042, fees 631.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why settle the account? The depot ceases on 31 December; Paul keeps the balance to pay the 2026 IGS with the closing return.

**What it changes.** 521 balance at December 31: 0 FCFA.

**Where it is filed.** Statement and reconciliation in the Bank binder.

**See again:** Accounting M29 “Organisation and accounting controls”.

- No monthly return: under the IGS everything is filed once a year.

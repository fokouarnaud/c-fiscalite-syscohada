### Saturday 5 September: the first delivery

The brewery truck delivers 235 crates of 65 cl at 6,160 FCFA before tax. Paul pays 1,798,643 FCFA by transfer, VAT and withholding included.

[[piece:S-B2-09-002]]

**The rule.** Purchases 6011 at full cost; 5% withholding for an IGS customer; VAT not recoverable.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Reminder: VAT and withholding stay in the cost.

**What it changes.** A 1,798,643 FCFA payment; the stock card gains 235 crates.

**Where it is filed.** Invoice in the Payments binder with the signed delivery note; a line in the book.

**See again:** Accounting M6 “Purchases”; Taxation M1 “The tax system and regimes”.

### Wednesday 30 September: cash sales

At the counter Paul and Brigitte sold 314 crates for cash, full against empty: 2,637,600 FCFA.

[[piece:S-B2-09-004]]

**The rule.** Receipts 7011 in the till 5711, no VAT.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why track the running total? Above 50 million in the year, the depot would be moved to the actual regime.

**What it changes.** Receipts grow, and the till, until the deposit.

**Where it is filed.** Cash book in the Receipts binder.

**See again:** Accounting M7 “Sales”; Accounting M1 “The OHADA accounting framework”.

### Wednesday 30 September: Orange Money payments

One customer in three pays by phone: 1,310,400 FCFA this month; the operator takes its fees and the balance goes to the bank.

[[piece:S-B2-09-005]]

**The rule.** 552, fees 631, 585.

> **Why this choice and not another.** Why 585? It avoids counting the same money twice.

**What it changes.** Orange Money receipts reach the bank.

**Where it is filed.** Printed statement in the Bank and Mobile Money binder.

**See again:** Accounting M12 “Settlements”.

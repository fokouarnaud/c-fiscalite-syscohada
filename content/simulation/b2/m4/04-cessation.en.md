At the end of September, Mrs Eyango, bursar of a Bonapriso hotel, offers Paul to supply all its drinks from January 2027. With this contract the depot would turn over about 75 million: well above the 50 million IGS threshold [[cgi:art. 93 quater]].

Keeping the sole proprietorship would mean crossing the threshold during 2027 and a forced move to the actual regime [[cgi:art. C 39]]. Paul picks a clean date: the business ceases on 31 December 2026 and is contributed to an LLC trading under the actual regime from 1 January 2027.

Any cessation is notified at least three months ahead [[cgi:art. 95]]: by 30 September for 31 December. Paul files on the 25th.

[[piece:S-B2-09-001]]

> **Why this choice and not another.** Waiting until January would be too late. The notice also opens a review of the business's tax position [[cgi:art. 95 bis]].

**What it changes.** The last quarter prepares the LLC; the depot keeps selling until 31 December.

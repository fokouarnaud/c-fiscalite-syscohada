## Funding equipment

An **investment grant** funds the purchase of a fixed asset. It is shown in equity, in account [[syscohada:14]] (for example [[syscohada:141]] for an equipment grant) [[guide:2e partie]].

It is then **released to income** at the pace of the funded asset's depreciation: each year, debit 14 and credit [[syscohada:799]] (reversals of investment grants).

Mbarga Distribution receives 5,000,000 CFA francs to fund a lorry depreciated over 5 years: an annual release of 1,000,000.

| Entry | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt | 521 (5,000,000) | 141 (5,000,000) |
| Annual release | 141 (1,000,000) | 799 (1,000,000) |

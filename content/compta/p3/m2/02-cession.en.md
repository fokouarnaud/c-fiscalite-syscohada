## Gain or loss

On sale, the securities leave at their purchase cost. The difference with the sale price is:

- a **gain**, credited to [[syscohada:777]] (gains on disposals of short-term securities);
- a **loss**, debited to [[syscohada:677]] (losses on short-term securities) [[guide:2e partie]].

Mbarga Distribution bought 100 shares at 10,000 CFA francs, 1,000,000 in total ([[syscohada:502]]). It sells them for 1,200,000:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| 521 Bank | 1,200,000 | |
| 502 Shares | | 1,000,000 |
| 777 Gains on disposals of short-term securities | | 200,000 |

## Why does the business hold these securities?

| Intention | Category | Account |
|---|---|---|
| Exert influence or control over another company | Equity investments | [[syscohada:26]] |
| Invest funds over the long term, without seeking control | Long-term securities | [[syscohada:274]] |
| Place available cash for a short time | Short-term investment securities | [[syscohada:50]] |

Equity investments and long-term securities are **financial assets**; short-term investment securities belong to **cash** [[guide:2e partie]].

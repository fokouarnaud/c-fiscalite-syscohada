## Unsettled debts and receivables

At year end, foreign currency debts and receivables are **restated at the closing rate**. The difference is not yet realised:

- unrealised loss (debt up, receivable down): translation difference, assets [[syscohada:478]], and a **provision** for exchange loss [[syscohada:194]] under prudence;
- unrealised gain: translation difference, liabilities [[syscohada:479]], **without** income [[guide:2e partie]].

Example: the 10,000 USD debt booked at 600 (6,000,000) is worth 6,200,000 at the closing rate of 620.

| Entry | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Translation difference | 478 (200,000) | 401 (200,000) |
| Provision | 697 (200,000) | 194 (200,000) |

## The exchange rate on the transaction date

A foreign currency invoice is converted into CFA francs at the **rate on the transaction date** [[guide:2e partie]]. By settlement, the rate may have moved:

- settling costs more (debt) or brings in less (receivable): **exchange loss** in [[syscohada:676]];
- the reverse: **exchange gain** in [[syscohada:776]].

The euro is an exception: its parity with the CFA franc is fixed (€1 = 655.957 CFA francs), so no exchange difference arises on a euro transaction.

Example: Mbarga Distribution buys 10,000 USD of goods at 600 CFA francs, i.e. 6,000,000. It pays at 610: 6,100,000, an exchange loss of 100,000.

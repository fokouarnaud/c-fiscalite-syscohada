## Building for itself

When the business builds an asset for its own use, its costs (materials, wages) first go to class 6. On completion, the production cost is taken to assets and **own work capitalised** is recorded as income, in account [[syscohada:722]] for a tangible asset [[guide:1re partie, ch. 5]].

Mbarga Distribution builds its own warehouse for 6,000,000 CFA francs:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| 231 Buildings | 6,000,000 | |
| 722 Own work capitalised, tangible assets | | 6,000,000 |

The income in 722 offsets the year's expenses: the result is neither inflated nor reduced by the construction.

## A fixed asset, a long-lived item

A fixed asset is an item the business keeps for lasting use, beyond one year: equipment, vehicles, buildings, software [[guide:1re partie, ch. 5]].

Its **cost** includes the purchase price and every cost incurred to bring the item into working condition: transport, installation, assembly. Recoverable VAT is not part of it: it goes to [[syscohada:4451]] (recoverable VAT on fixed assets).

| Item | Account |
|---|---|
| Software and websites | [[syscohada:213]] |
| Buildings on own land | [[syscohada:231]] |
| Office and computer equipment and furniture | [[syscohada:244]] |
| Transport equipment | [[syscohada:245]] |

The debt to the seller of a fixed asset goes to the suppliers of fixed assets account [[syscohada:481]].

## The bank loan

The capital received is a financial debt, in account [[syscohada:162]]. Each instalment comprises:

- **interest**, a financial expense in [[syscohada:671]];
- **capital repayment**, which reduces the debt in 162 [[guide:2e partie]].

## The level instalment

For a loan of 10,000,000 CFA francs at 10% over 5 years, the level instalment is 10,000,000 × 0.10 / (1 − 1.10⁻⁵) ≈ **2,637,975 CFA francs**.

| Year | Interest | Capital repaid | Instalment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,000,000 | 1,637,975 | 2,637,975 |

Interest falls each year while the capital share rises.

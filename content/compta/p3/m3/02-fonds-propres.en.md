## Partners' contributions

- Capital increase: share capital [[syscohada:101]] increases.
- Current account advance: the partner lends to the company, a debt in [[syscohada:462]].

## Lease-purchase

The revised SYSCOHADA shows an asset under a finance lease or hire purchase as if bought on credit: the asset is debited, the lease-purchase liability credited to [[syscohada:17]] (for example [[syscohada:173]] for equipment leasing). Each payment is then split into interest and repayment of the liability [[guide:2e partie]].

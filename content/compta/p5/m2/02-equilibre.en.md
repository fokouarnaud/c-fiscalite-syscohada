## Working capital, financing need, cash

- **Working capital (FR)** = long-term funds (equity + financial debts) − fixed assets.
- **Financing need (BF)** = current assets − current liabilities.
- **Net cash (TN)** = cash assets − cash liabilities = FR − BF [[guide:3e partie]].

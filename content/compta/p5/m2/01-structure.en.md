## The normal system model

| Assets | Liabilities and equity |
|---|---|
| Fixed assets (net of depreciation and impairment) | Equity and similar funds |
| Current assets (inventories, receivables, including non-ordinary) | Financial debts and similar funds |
| Cash assets | Current liabilities (suppliers, tax and social debts, non-ordinary) |
| Translation differences, assets | Cash liabilities |
| | Translation differences, liabilities |

Each asset line is shown **gross**, with its depreciation and impairment, then **net** [[guide:3e partie]].

Income tax for the year, not yet paid, is a current liability ([[syscohada:441]]), recorded by debiting [[syscohada:891]].

## Where does the money come from, where does it go?

The **cash flow statement** (TFT) explains the change in net cash between two balance sheets, in three groups [[guide:3e partie]]:

| Flows | Examples |
|---|---|
| Operating activities | Receipts from customers, payments to suppliers and staff, taxes |
| Investing activities | Purchases and sales of fixed assets and equity investments |
| Financing activities | Loans received and repaid, contributions, dividends paid ([[syscohada:465]]) |

**Change in net cash = operating flows + investing flows + financing flows.**

## A worked example

- Operating flows: +3,000,000 CFA francs.
- Investing flows: purchase of a lorry, −15,000,000.
- Financing flows: loan received +10,000,000.

Change in net cash: 3,000,000 − 15,000,000 + 10,000,000 = **−2,000,000 CFA francs**.

Overall self-financing capacity (CAFG) starts from the GOS and ignores depreciation, which involves no cash outflow [[guide:3e partie]]. Paying dividends ([[syscohada:465]] debited, [[syscohada:521]] credited) is a financing flow.

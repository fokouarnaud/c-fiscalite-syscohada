## Organising data entry

The business keeps **one journal per type of transaction**: purchases, sales, bank, cash, miscellaneous. Subsidiary journals are centralised each month, and each entry refers to its supporting document [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]].

Entities below the thresholds set by the Uniform Act may use the **minimal cash system**; the others apply the **normal system**.

## Checking

- The **trial balance** checks that debits equal credits.
- **Matching** pairs invoices with payments in third-party accounts ([[syscohada:401]], [[syscohada:411]]).
- **Physical counts** compare book inventory and cash with what actually exists.

## Comparing the books and the statement

Each month, account [[syscohada:521]] is compared with the bank statement. Differences come from transactions recorded on one side only [[guide:1re partie, ch. 4]].

Mbarga Distribution, end of December: book balance 1,250,000 CFA francs; statement 1,330,000. Differences:

- bank charges of 20,000 not yet recorded;
- a customer transfer of 100,000 received by the bank, not yet recorded.

Corrected book balance: 1,250,000 − 20,000 + 100,000 = **1,330,000 CFA francs**, equal to the statement.

| Adjusting entry | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Bank charges | 631 (20,000) | 521 (20,000) |
| Customer transfer | 521 (100,000) | 411 (100,000) |

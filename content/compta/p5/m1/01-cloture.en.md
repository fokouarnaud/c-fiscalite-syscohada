## From the last day to the first

At year end, after the closing adjustments, expense accounts (classes 6 and 8) and income accounts (classes 7 and 8) are cleared: the difference is the **result**, a profit in [[syscohada:131]] or a loss in [[syscohada:139]] [[guide:3e partie]].

On reopening, the opening balance sheet takes over the closing one (continuity), then adjustments are **reversed**: a prepaid expense becomes an expense of the new year.

Example, reversing the prepaid insurance premium (900,000 CFA francs): debit [[syscohada:625]], credit [[syscohada:476]].

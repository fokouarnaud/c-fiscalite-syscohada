## The partners' decision

The partners' meeting decides how the profit is appropriated [[guide:3e partie]]:

- **legal reserve** [[syscohada:111]]: in OHADA commercial companies, at least one tenth of the profit, until it reaches one fifth of the capital;
- **other reserves** [[syscohada:118]];
- **dividends** payable to partners [[syscohada:465]];
- the rest as **retained earnings** [[syscohada:121]].

Mbarga Distribution: profit 4,500,000; legal reserve 450,000; dividends 2,000,000; retained earnings 2,050,000.

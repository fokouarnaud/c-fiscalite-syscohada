## From turnover to net result

The SYSCOHADA income statement shows successive balances [[guide:3e partie]]:

| Balance | Computation | Account |
|---|---|---|
| Gross trading margin | Sales of goods − cost of goods sold | [[syscohada:132]] |
| Value added | Output and margin − external consumption | [[syscohada:133]] |
| Gross operating surplus | Value added − staff costs | [[syscohada:134]] |
| Operating result | GOS + reversals and transfers − depreciation and provisions | [[syscohada:135]] |
| Financial result | Financial income − financial expenses | [[syscohada:136]] |
| Result from ordinary activities | Operating result + financial result | [[syscohada:137]] |
| Non-ordinary result | Non-ordinary income − non-ordinary expenses | [[syscohada:138]] |
| Net result | ROA + non-ordinary result − profit sharing − tax | [[syscohada:13]] |

## A step-by-step computation

Sales of goods 42,000,000; purchases 30,000,000; opening inventory 2,000,000; closing inventory 2,600,000; rent and external services 4,800,000; staff costs 2,700,000; depreciation 1,500,000.

1. Cost of goods sold: 30,000,000 + 2,000,000 − 2,600,000 = 29,400,000.
2. **Gross trading margin**: 42,000,000 − 29,400,000 = 12,600,000.
3. **Value added**: 12,600,000 − 4,800,000 = 7,800,000.
4. **GOS**: 7,800,000 − 2,700,000 = 5,100,000.
5. **Operating result**: 5,100,000 − 1,500,000 = 3,600,000 CFA francs.

Income statement accounts are cleared by crediting expense accounts and debiting income accounts, the result going to [[syscohada:131]] [[guide:3e partie]].

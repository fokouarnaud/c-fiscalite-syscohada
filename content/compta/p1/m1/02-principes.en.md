## The principles behind every entry

The revised SYSCOHADA rests on conventions that the accountant applies before even choosing an account [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]].

| Principle | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Prudence | Do not overstate assets and income, nor understate liabilities and expenses: a probable loss is recognised, a probable gain waits until it is earned. |
| Going concern | Value assets as if the business will continue operating. |
| Consistency | Keep the same methods from one year to the next, unless a change is justified and explained in the notes. |
| Historical cost | Record an asset at its purchase or production cost. |
| Accrual (period matching) | Attach to each financial year the expenses and income that belong to it, and only those. |
| Opening balance sheet continuity | The opening balance sheet of a year is the closing balance sheet of the previous year. |
| No offsetting | Never offset a debt against a receivable, nor an expense against income. |
| Materiality | Highlight any information that may influence the judgement of readers of the financial statements. |
| Substance over form | Reflect the economic reality of a transaction rather than only its legal form. |

## An example at Mbarga Distribution

On 31 December, a customer of Mbarga Distribution disputes an invoice of 2,000,000 CFA francs and may not pay. Prudence requires recognising an impairment now, in an impairment account for customer receivables [[syscohada:491]], without waiting for the dispute to end.

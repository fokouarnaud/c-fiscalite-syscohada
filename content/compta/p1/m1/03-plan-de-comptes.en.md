## Nine classes of accounts

The revised SYSCOHADA chart of accounts places each account in a **class**, given by its first digit [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]]. Each additional digit narrows the account: [[syscohada:4]] third-party accounts, then [[syscohada:44]] State, then [[syscohada:445]] State, recoverable VAT, then [[syscohada:4452]].

| Class | Content | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Long-term funding (equity, borrowings) | Balance sheet, liabilities |
| 2 | Fixed assets | Balance sheet, assets |
| 3 | Inventories | Balance sheet, assets |
| 4 | Third parties (suppliers, customers, staff, State) | Balance sheet, assets or liabilities |
| 5 | Cash | Balance sheet, assets or liabilities |
| 6 | Expenses from ordinary activities | Income statement |
| 7 | Income from ordinary activities | Income statement |
| 8 | Other expenses and other income | Income statement |
| 9 | Off-balance-sheet commitments and management accounting | Outside the financial statements |

## Double entry

Every transaction affects **at least two accounts**: one is debited, the other credited, and total debits always equal total credits.

Mbarga Distribution buys goods on credit for 1,000,000 CFA francs excluding tax. VAT at the overall rate of [[param:tva.taux_global]] amounts to 192,500 CFA francs (2026 financial year):

| Account | Label | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[syscohada:601]] | Purchases of goods | 1,000,000 | |
| [[syscohada:4452]] | Recoverable VAT on purchases | 192,500 | |
| [[syscohada:401]] | Suppliers | | 1,192,500 |

Debits (1,192,500) equal credits (1,192,500): the entry balances.

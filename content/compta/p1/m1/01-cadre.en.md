## A common accounting law across the OHADA area

Cameroon is a member of OHADA, the Organisation for the Harmonisation of Business Law in Africa. Its businesses keep their books under the **Uniform Act on accounting law and financial reporting** (AUDCIF), whose technical part is the OHADA accounting system (SYSCOHADA).

The **revised** SYSCOHADA applies to the individual accounts of entities for financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2018, and to consolidated accounts from 1 January 2019. The OHADA implementation guide explains how each account works [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]].

## What the Uniform Act requires

- Accounts that are **regular, faithful and transparent**, giving a true and fair view of assets, financial position and results.
- A **financial year** of twelve months, in principle the calendar year.
- Annual **financial statements** following the SYSCOHADA models: balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement and notes.
- A common **chart of accounts** organised in nine classes.

## Two systems depending on size

| System | Who uses it | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| Normal system | Most entities | Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, notes |
| Minimal cash system (SMT) | Very small entities, below the revenue thresholds set by the Uniform Act | A simplified statement based on receipts and payments |

In this course, the running example is **Mbarga Distribution SARL**, a fictitious wholesaler based in Douala that uses the normal system. Its purchases of goods are recorded, for example, in account [[syscohada:601]].

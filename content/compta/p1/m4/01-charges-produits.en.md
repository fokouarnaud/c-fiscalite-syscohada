## The income statement, a film of the year

The income statement describes a year's activity: the **income** earned and the **expenses** incurred to earn it [[guide:3e partie]].

| Class | Content | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Expenses from ordinary activities | Purchases [[syscohada:601]], rents [[syscohada:622]], wages [[syscohada:661]] |
| 7 | Income from ordinary activities | Sales [[syscohada:701]], interest received [[syscohada:771]] |
| 8 | Other expenses and other income | Disposals of fixed assets, expenses and income outside ordinary activities |

An expense is recorded on the **debit** side, income on the **credit** side.

## The result

**Result = income − expenses.** If positive, it is a profit, recorded in account [[syscohada:131]]; if negative, a loss, recorded in account [[syscohada:139]].

## Example: Mbarga Distribution's first year

| Income | Amount | Expenses | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales of goods | 42,000,000 | Purchases of goods | 30,000,000 |
| | | Rents | 4,800,000 |
| | | Wages | 2,700,000 |
| **Total** | **42,000,000** | **Total** | **37,500,000** |

The profit is 4,500,000 CFA francs.

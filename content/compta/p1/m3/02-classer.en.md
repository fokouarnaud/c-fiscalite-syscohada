## Classifying a balance sheet item

Two questions are usually enough [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]]:

1. **Use or source of funds?** What the business owns or is owed is an asset; what it owes or what the partners contributed is a liability or equity.
2. **Long-term or current?** An asset used over several years is fixed; an asset or receivable that turns over in the operating cycle is current.

| Item | Heading | Account |
|---|---|---|
| Office computer | Fixed assets | [[syscohada:244]] |
| Goods in store | Current assets | [[syscohada:311]] |
| Unpaid customer invoice | Current assets | [[syscohada:411]] |
| Unpaid supplier invoice | Current liabilities | [[syscohada:401]] |
| Five-year loan | Financial debts | [[syscohada:162]] |
| Bank overdraft | Cash liabilities | [[syscohada:561]] |

The same account can change sides: a bank account with a debit balance is a cash asset, one with a credit balance (overdraft) is a cash liability.

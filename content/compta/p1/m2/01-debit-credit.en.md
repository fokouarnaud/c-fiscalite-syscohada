## Debit or credit?

Every account has two columns: the **debit** on the left, the **credit** on the right. Which side an entry goes to depends on the nature of the account [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]].

| Nature of the account | It increases on the… | It decreases on the… | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset (uses: goods, receivables, cash) | debit | credit | Bank [[syscohada:521]] |
| Liability (sources: capital, debts) | credit | debit | Suppliers [[syscohada:401]] |
| Expense | debit | — | Rents [[syscohada:622]] |
| Income | credit | — | Sales of goods [[syscohada:701]] |

## The method in three questions

1. Which accounts does the transaction affect?
2. Does each one increase or decrease?
3. Which side does each amount go to, so that total debits equal total credits?

## Example: Mbarga Distribution pays a supplier

Mbarga Distribution pays a supplier debt of 350,000 CFA francs by transfer. The debt decreases: **debit** [[syscohada:401]]. The bank balance decreases: **credit** [[syscohada:521]].

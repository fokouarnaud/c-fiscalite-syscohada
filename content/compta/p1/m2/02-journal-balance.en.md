## From document to trial balance

Every entry starts from a **supporting document** (invoice, bank statement, payslip) that keeps its trace [[guide:1re partie, ch. 1]].

1. **The journal** records transactions in date order: date, accounts, label, debit and credit amounts, document reference.
2. **The general ledger** groups all journal lines account by account: it gives the balance of each account.
3. **The trial balance** lists every account with its totals and balances. Total debits equal total credits: if not, an entry is unbalanced or wrongly posted.

## The balance of an account

- **Debit** balance: total debits exceed total credits (normal for an asset or expense account).
- **Credit** balance: total credits exceed total debits (normal for a liability or income account).

## Example: Mbarga Distribution's cash account

Account [[syscohada:571]] receives 450,000 CFA francs of customer receipts on the debit side and 120,000 CFA francs of spending on the credit side. Its debit balance is 330,000 CFA francs: the cash on hand.

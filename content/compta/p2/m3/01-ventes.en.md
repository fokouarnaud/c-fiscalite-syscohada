## The sales invoice

A sale is recorded on delivery, which is also the VAT taxable event [[cgi:art. 133]]. Income goes to class 7, VAT to [[syscohada:443]], the receivable to [[syscohada:411]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

| Sale of… | Account |
|---|---|
| Goods | [[syscohada:701]] |
| Finished goods | [[syscohada:702]] |
| Services | [[syscohada:706]] |
| Carriage and costs re-invoiced to the customer | [[syscohada:7071]] |

Mbarga Distribution sells goods on credit for 1,500,000 CFA francs and re-invoices 50,000 of carriage. VAT base: 1,550,000; VAT: 298,375; total: 1,848,375.

## The deposit, seller's side

When Mbarga Distribution delivers in returnable crates, it charges the customer a deposit. It is a **debt** to the customer, refundable on return, in account [[syscohada:4194]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

| Transaction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit invoiced (20,000) | 411 | 4194 |
| Crates returned (20,000) | 4194 | 411 |

A cash sale is settled at once: cash [[syscohada:571]] or bank [[syscohada:521]] replaces the customer account.

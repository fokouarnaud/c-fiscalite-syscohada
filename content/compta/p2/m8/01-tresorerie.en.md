Collecting a customer receivable: debit cash or bank, credit [[syscohada:411]]. Paying a supplier: debit [[syscohada:401]], credit cash or bank [[guide:1re partie, ch. 4]].

## Internal transfers

To fill the cash box from the bank, the fund transfer account [[syscohada:585]] is used: it avoids recording the transaction twice when bank and cash are kept in different journals.

## Paying later with a bill

A **bill of exchange** (draft, promissory note) records that a debt will be paid on a set date [[guide:1re partie, ch. 4]].

| Transaction | At the customer (debtor) | At the supplier (creditor) |
|---|---|---|
| Acceptance of the bill | 401 → [[syscohada:402]] Bills payable | 411 → [[syscohada:412]] Bills receivable |
| Payment at maturity | 402 debited, bank credited | Bank debited, 412 credited |

Example: a customer of Mbarga Distribution accepts a bill of 1,192,500 CFA francs. Mbarga Distribution debits 412 and credits 411, then at maturity debits 521 and credits 412.

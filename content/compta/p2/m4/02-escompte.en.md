## A financial reduction

A **cash discount** rewards early payment. It is a **financial** reduction: it is always recorded, even when shown on the invoice [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

- Buyer's side: discount received, financial income, credited to [[syscohada:773]].
- Seller's side: discount granted, financial expense, debited to [[syscohada:673]].

VAT is computed on the **net amount after the cash discount**.

Buyer's example: gross 1,000,000; 2% discount = 20,000; net 980,000; VAT 188,650; total 1,168,650.

|---|---|---|

| 4452 Recoverable VAT | 188,650 | |
| 773 Cash discounts received | | 20,000 |
| 401 Suppliers | | 1,168,650 |

## Discount, allowance, rebate

- **Trade discount**: a reduction for quantity or loyalty.
- **Allowance**: a reduction for poor quality or late delivery.
- **Rebate**: a reduction based on a period's turnover.

Granted **on the invoice**, they are not recorded: the net amount is booked directly, and VAT is computed on that net [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

Granted **after the invoice**, by a credit note, the buyer records them as a credit to [[syscohada:6019]] (discounts, allowances and rebates received), reversing the recoverable VAT in [[syscohada:4452]].

Example: invoice of 1,000,000 CFA francs with a 10% discount; net 900,000; VAT 173,250; total 1,073,250.

## Who pays, and for what?

| Situation | Account |
|---|---|
| Transport or forwarding paid to **buy** goods | [[syscohada:6015]] Purchase costs |
| Transport paid to a carrier to **deliver** to customers | [[syscohada:612]] Transport on sales |
| Carriage **re-invoiced** to the customer | [[syscohada:7071]] |
| Staff travel | [[syscohada:618]] |

Purchase costs attached to goods add to the purchase cost; delivery costs to customers are distribution expenses [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

VAT paid to a carrier is recovered in [[syscohada:4454]] (VAT on external services); that of a forwarding agent tied to the purchase follows the purchase in [[syscohada:4452]].

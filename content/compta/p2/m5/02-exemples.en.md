## At Mbarga Distribution

1. A forwarding agent invoices 120,000 CFA francs excl. tax to clear a batch of goods: purchase costs [[syscohada:6015]], VAT 23,100 in [[syscohada:4452]], debt 143,100.
2. A carrier invoices 80,000 excl. tax to deliver a customer, paid by transfer: transport on sales [[syscohada:612]], VAT 15,400 in [[syscohada:4454]], bank credited with 95,400.

If Mbarga Distribution re-invoices this transport to the customer, it records income in [[syscohada:7071]]; the expense in 612 stays recorded.

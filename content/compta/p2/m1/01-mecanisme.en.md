## A consumption tax collected by the business

A VAT-registered business **collects** VAT on its sales and **deducts** the VAT paid on its purchases: it pays the State only the difference. The final cost falls on the consumer.

- **Taxable event**: delivery of the goods for a sale, performance of the service for a service [[cgi:art. 133]].
- **Chargeability**: for a delivery, at the taxable event; an advance received makes the tax chargeable up to its amount [[cgi:art. 134]].
- **Rate**: standard rate of 17.5%, plus 10% additional council cents, giving an overall rate of [[param:tva.taux_global]] [[cgi:art. 142]].

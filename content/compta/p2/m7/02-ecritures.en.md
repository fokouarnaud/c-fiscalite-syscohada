| Entry | Debit | Credit |

| Gross pay and withholdings | 661 Pay (500,000) | 431 CNPS (21,000), 447 Taxes withheld (55,450), 422 Net pay (423,550) |

Employer and employee CNPS contributions come from social security law: their rates are not in the Tax Code, and exercises give them as amounts. The employer's Land Credit contribution (2.5%) is a payroll tax, in account [[syscohada:641]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 3]].

## What the employer withholds

From gross pay, the employer withholds and then pays over:

- the **employee CNPS contribution**, due to social security [[syscohada:431]];
- **personal income tax** and its council cents: 30% professional expenses (capped at 4,800,000 a year), pension contribution deducted [[cgi:art. 34]], allowance of 500,000 a year [[cgi:art. 29]], scale from 10% to 35% [[cgi:art. 69]], plus 10% council cents [[cgi:art. 71]];
- the **Land Credit contribution** (1% employee share), the **audiovisual fee** and the **local development tax** [[cgi:art. C 86]].

Tax withholdings go to [[syscohada:447]] (taxes withheld at source), net pay to [[syscohada:422]].

## What the business earns beside its trade

A wholesaler that sublets part of its warehouse, or lends staff, earns **ancillary income** [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]]:

| Income | Account |
|---|---|
| Rentals | [[syscohada:7073]] |
| Commissions and brokerage | [[syscohada:7072]] |
| Provision of staff | [[syscohada:7075]] |
| Insurance indemnities received | [[syscohada:7582]] |

Mbarga Distribution receives 250,000 CFA francs of rent by transfer for a sublet unit (no VAT in this example): debit [[syscohada:521]], credit [[syscohada:7073]].

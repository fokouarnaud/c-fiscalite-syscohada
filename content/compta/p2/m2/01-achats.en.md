## The purchase invoice

A purchase is recorded on receipt of the invoice: the item as a class 6 expense, VAT in [[syscohada:4452]], the debt in [[syscohada:401]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

| Purchase of… | Account |
|---|---|
| Goods for resale | [[syscohada:601]] |
| Raw materials | [[syscohada:602]] |
| Stored consumable materials and supplies | [[syscohada:604]] |
| Non-stocked supplies (water, electricity, small tools) | [[syscohada:605]] |

## Incidental costs

Costs invoiced to bring in or clear the purchase (transport, forwarding, insurance) are **purchase costs**, in account [[syscohada:6015]] for goods. They are part of the VAT base.

Mbarga Distribution buys 2,000,000 CFA francs of goods; the supplier also invoices 100,000 of transport. VAT base: 2,100,000; VAT: 404,250; total: 2,504,250.

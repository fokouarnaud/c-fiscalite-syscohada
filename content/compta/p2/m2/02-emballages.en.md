## Deposit, then return

A supplier may charge a **deposit** on returnable packaging (pallets, crates): it invoices them, then refunds the deposit when they come back. The deposit is not an expense but a receivable from the supplier, in account [[syscohada:4094]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 2]].

| Transaction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit invoiced (30,000) | 4094 | 401 |
| Packaging returned (30,000) | 401 | 4094 |

**Non-returnable** packaging (cartons thrown away with the goods) is a purchase, in account [[syscohada:608]].

## Revising the amount

At each year end, the provision is adjusted to the estimated risk: an increase gives an additional charge, a decrease a **reversal** credited to [[syscohada:791]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

A former employee sues Mbarga Distribution. At end 2026 the probable loss is 2,000,000 CFA francs; at end 2027 the lawyer estimates it at 1,200,000.

| Year | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| 2026: charge | 691 (2,000,000) | 191 (2,000,000) |
| 2027: reversal of 800,000 | 191 (800,000) | 791 (800,000) |

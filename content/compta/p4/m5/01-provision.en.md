## The principle

A **provision for risks and charges** is a liability whose amount or timing is uncertain, but whose outflow of resources is **probable** at year end: litigation, warranties given to customers, fines [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

- Charge: [[syscohada:691]] (operating) or [[syscohada:697]] (financial).
- Provision in liabilities: [[syscohada:19]] (for example [[syscohada:191]] for litigation, [[syscohada:192]] for warranties).

A merely **possible** risk is not provided for: it is disclosed in the notes.

## Two entries, outside ordinary activities

A fixed asset disposal is a **non-ordinary** transaction (HAO) [[guide:1re partie, ch. 5]]:

1. the **sale price** is income, in [[syscohada:822]] (proceeds from disposals of tangible assets);
2. **removing the asset**: its cost and accumulated depreciation are cancelled, and the NBV becomes an expense in [[syscohada:812]] (carrying amount of disposals).

**Gain = sale price − NBV.**

Before the disposal, the year's depreciation up to the disposal date is recorded.

If an asset whose VAT was deducted is sold before the end of its fourth year without VAT, part of the deducted tax must be repaid [[cgi:art. 145]].

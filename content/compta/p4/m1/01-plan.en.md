## Recording wear and tear

Depreciation spreads the cost of a fixed asset over its useful life [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]]. Each year, the **charge** is an expense in [[syscohada:681]]; accumulated depreciation is deducted from assets, in [[syscohada:28]] (for example [[syscohada:284]] for equipment).

- **Straight-line**: a constant charge, prorated in the first year from the date in service.
- **Declining balance**: the straight-line rate times a coefficient, on the net value; switch to straight-line when it becomes higher.

**Net book value (NBV) = cost − accumulated depreciation.**

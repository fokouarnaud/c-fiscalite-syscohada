## At year end

| Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| 681 Operating depreciation charges | 1,500,000 | |
| 284 Depreciation of equipment | | 1,500,000 |

In the first year, the lorry bought in April is depreciated over 9 months only: 10,000,000 / 5 × 9/12 = 1,500,000 CFA francs. In later years, the charge is 2,000,000, and the final year covers the remaining 3 months [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

Depreciation involves no cash outflow: it is a **calculated** expense that reduces the result without touching cash.

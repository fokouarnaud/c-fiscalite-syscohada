## A customer who may not pay

When a customer disputes or pays abnormally late, the receivable is transferred to **doubtful receivables**: debit [[syscohada:416]], credit [[syscohada:411]], for the amount including tax [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

The **impairment** is computed on the amount **excluding tax**, since VAT can be adjusted if the loss becomes final: debit [[syscohada:659]], credit [[syscohada:491]].

Example: a receivable of 1,192,500 CFA francs including tax (1,000,000 excl. tax); estimated loss 40%; impairment: 400,000.

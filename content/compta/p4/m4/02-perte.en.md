## When the receivable is uncollectible

The loss is recorded in [[syscohada:651]] (losses on customer receivables) and the receivable leaves 416. The impairment no longer needed is reversed: debit 491, credit [[syscohada:759]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

Example (non-VAT transaction): a doubtful receivable of 500,000 CFA francs impaired by 300,000, declared uncollectible.

| Entry | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Loss | 651 (500,000) | 416 (500,000) |
| Reversal | 491 (300,000) | 759 (300,000) |

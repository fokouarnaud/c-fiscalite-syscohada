## What belongs to next year

The accrual principle requires taking out of the result what belongs to the following year [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]]:

- **Prepaid expense**: an expense paid or invoiced, partly for next year; debit [[syscohada:476]].
- **Deferred income**: income invoiced, partly for next year; credit [[syscohada:477]].

Example: on 1 October, Mbarga Distribution pays an annual insurance premium of 1,200,000 CFA francs. Nine months belong to next year: a prepaid expense of 900,000 (debit 476, credit 625). The entry is reversed when the next year opens.

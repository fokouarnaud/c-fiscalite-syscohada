## What belongs to the year, without an invoice

- **Accrued expense**: service received, invoice not yet arrived; credit [[syscohada:408]] (suppliers, invoices not yet received), VAT in [[syscohada:4455]].
- **Accrued income**: goods delivered, invoice not yet issued; debit [[syscohada:418]] (customers, invoices to be issued), VAT in [[syscohada:4435]] [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

Examples at 31 December:

| Transaction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| December electricity, 100,000 excl. tax, invoice not received | 605 (100,000), 4455 (19,250) | 408 (119,250) |
| Delivery of 500,000 excl. tax not invoiced | 418 (596,250) | 701 (500,000), 4435 (96,250) |

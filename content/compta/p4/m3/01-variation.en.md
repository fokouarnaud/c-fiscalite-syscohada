## Periodic inventory

During the year, purchases go to expenses (class 6). At year end, the **change in inventories** corrects them [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]]:

1. reverse opening inventory: debit [[syscohada:6031]], credit [[syscohada:31]];
2. record closing inventory: debit 31, credit 6031.

**Cost of goods sold = purchases + opening inventory − closing inventory.**

Mbarga Distribution: purchases 10,000,000; opening inventory 2,000,000; closing inventory 2,600,000. Cost of goods sold: 9,400,000 CFA francs.

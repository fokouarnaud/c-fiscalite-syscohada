## Weighted average or first in, first out

Issues from inventory are valued at **weighted average unit cost** (CMUP) or **first in, first out** (FIFO, PEPS) [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

## Impairment

If an inventory item's recoverable value (selling price less selling costs) is below its cost, an impairment is recorded: debit [[syscohada:659]], credit [[syscohada:39]] (for example [[syscohada:391]] for goods).

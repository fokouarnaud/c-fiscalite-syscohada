## When value falls

At year end, if a fixed asset's **recoverable value** (what it is worth to the business today) is below its **NBV**, the difference is an **impairment** [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

- Charge: [[syscohada:691]] (operating provisions and impairment).
- Accumulated impairment: [[syscohada:29]], deducted from assets (for example [[syscohada:294]] for equipment).

Unlike depreciation, impairment can be **reversed** if value recovers: the reversal is income in [[syscohada:791]].

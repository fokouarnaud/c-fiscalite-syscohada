## At Mbarga Distribution

A forklift has an NBV of 6,500,000 CFA francs, but repeated breakdowns bring its recoverable value down to 5,000,000. Impairment: 1,500,000.

| Entry | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Impairment | 691 (1,500,000) | 294 (1,500,000) |
| Partial reversal of 500,000 the next year | 294 (500,000) | 791 (500,000) |

Later depreciation charges are computed on the impaired value [[guide:1re partie, ch. 6]].

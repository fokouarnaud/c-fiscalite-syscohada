## A final withholding rate

Investment income (dividends, interest) bears a **final withholding rate of 15%** [[cgi:art. 70]], plus 10% council cents [[cgi:art. 71]], 16.5% in total.

- The rate falls to **10%** for dividends paid by businesses with turnover up to 3 billion CFA francs.
- It rises to **30%** for income paid to a person established in a tax haven.

Example: a partner of Mbarga Distribution (turnover below 3 billion) receives 1,000,000 CFA francs of dividends: tax 100,000, council cents 10,000, i.e. 110,000 withheld at source.

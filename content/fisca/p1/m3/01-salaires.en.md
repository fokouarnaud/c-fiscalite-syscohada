## From gross pay to taxable income

For an employee, income tax is computed over the year [[guide:1re partie, ch. 3]]:

1. gross income (salary and benefits);
2. less 30% **professional expenses**, capped at 4,800,000 CFA francs a year, and compulsory pension contributions paid to CNPS [[cgi:art. 34]];
3. less a flat **allowance** of 500,000 CFA francs [[cgi:art. 29]];
4. apply the **scale**: 10% up to 2,000,000; 15% from 2,000,001 to 3,000,000; 25% from 3,000,001 to 5,000,000; 35% above [[cgi:art. 69]];
5. add 10% additional council cents [[cgi:art. 71]].

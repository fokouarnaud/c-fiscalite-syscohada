## Who pays corporate tax?

Corporate income tax is charged on the profits of companies and other legal entities [[cgi:art. 2]]: public limited companies, limited liability companies (even single-member), cooperatives, industrial or commercial public bodies [[cgi:art. 3]].

## Taxable profit

Net taxable profit is computed after deducting all expenses **directly required by the business** in Cameroon: overheads, staff costs, rent, depreciation, justified provisions [[cgi:art. 7]]. It starts from the accounting result, adjusted by add-backs (non-deductible expenses) and deductions.

The tax return is filed by **15 March** (large companies unit), **15 April** (medium-sized companies centres) or **15 May** (local tax centres) [[cgi:art. 18]].

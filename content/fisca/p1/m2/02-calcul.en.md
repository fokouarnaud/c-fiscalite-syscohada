## The computation

- Standard rate: **[[param:is.taux]]** [[cgi:art. 17]]; reduced rate of **[[param:is.taux_reduit]]** for turnover up to 3 billion CFA francs [[cgi:art. 17 bis]].
- The fraction of profit below 1,000 CFA francs is disregarded.
- Additional council cents: 10% of the tax [[cgi:art. C 83]].

## The minimum tax

Corporate tax due cannot be lower than **[[param:is.taux_minimum_perception]]** of reference turnover, rounded down to the thousand [[cgi:art. 21]] [[cgi:art. 23]], plus 10% council cents. It is paid in monthly instalments, set against the year's corporate tax.

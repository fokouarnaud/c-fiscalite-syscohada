## Three books

Cameroon's General Tax Code (GTC), 2026 edition, updated for the 2026 finance law, brings together:

- **Book One**, taxes and duties: corporate tax, personal income tax, VAT, excise duties, registration and stamp duties;
- **Book Two**, the Tax Procedures Book: assessment, audit, collection, penalties, disputes;
- **Book Three**, local taxation: municipal taxes, additional council cents, regional taxes.

## Registering

Anyone liable to a tax must register within **15 working days** of starting business [[cgi:art. L 1]]. They receive a **unique identification number**, required on all documents of their transactions.

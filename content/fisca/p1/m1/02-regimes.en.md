## Two regimes depending on turnover

| Regime | Who? |
|---|---|
| General Synthetic Tax (IGS) | Annual turnover excluding tax below 50,000,000 CFA francs (commercial, industrial, craft, agro-pastoral activities) or 30,000,000 (liberal professions) |
| Actual (real) regime | Turnover at or above those thresholds, and some sectors whatever their turnover (oil, mining, gas, credit, microfinance, insurance, mobile telephony) |

These thresholds are set in [[cgi:art. 93 quater]]. A business whose turnover falls below them stays in its original regime for two years [[cgi:art. 93 quinquies]].

Mbarga Distribution, a wholesaler with 42,000,000 CFA francs of turnover in its first year, falls under the IGS; it will move to the actual regime once it reaches 50,000,000.

## The taxpayer's obligations

- Register within 15 working days of starting business [[cgi:art. L 1]] and show the unique identification number on documents [[cgi:art. L 1 bis]].
- File monthly returns for VAT, corporate tax instalments and payroll withholdings.
- File the annual tax return by 15 March, 15 April or 15 May depending on the tax centre [[cgi:art. 18]].
- Keep accounts in line with SYSCOHADA and retain supporting documents.

## Auditing and correcting

The tax authority checks returns, either as a desk audit or through an on-site accounting audit.

It can correct omissions, understatements and errors **until the end of the fourth year** following the year in which the tax became chargeable [[cgi:art. L 34]]. Taxpayers can also correct their own errors spontaneously.

Example: VAT due for 2026 can be reassessed until 31 December 2030.

Procedural documents are sent with acknowledgement of receipt; they are deemed received fifteen working days after dispatch to the declared address [[cgi:art. L 41]].

## The claim

A taxpayer who believes they were wrongly taxed may claim **in writing** within **30 days** of the issue of the collection notice or of becoming aware of the assessment [[cgi:art. L 116]].

The competent authority depends on the principal amount:

- head of the regional tax centre: up to 75,000,000 CFA francs;
- director of large companies: up to 200,000,000;
- director general of taxes: above that.

If the decision is not fully satisfactory, the taxpayer then turns to the minister in charge of finance [[cgi:art. L 118]], before going to court.

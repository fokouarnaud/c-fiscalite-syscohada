## Late interest

A reassessment for understatement, omission or inaccuracy triggers **late interest of 1.5% per month**, capped at **50%** of the duties [[cgi:art. L 95]]; any month started counts as a full month [[cgi:art. L 98]].

## Failure to file

A taxpayer who does not file after formal notice is **assessed ex officio**; the tax is increased by **100%** (150% for a repeat offence). A fine per unfiled return is also due: 200,000 CFA francs (large companies), 100,000 (medium-sized companies and specialised centres), 50,000 (local tax centres and individuals) [[cgi:art. L 97]].

Example: reassessed duties of 2,000,000 CFA francs, 10 months late: interest 1.5% × 10 = 15%, i.e. 300,000 CFA francs.

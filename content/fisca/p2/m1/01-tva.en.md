## Who, what?

Persons who habitually or occasionally carry out taxable transactions as part of an economic activity are liable to VAT [[cgi:art. 125]]: supplies of goods, services, imports [[cgi:art. 127]]. Some transactions are **exempt** (for example basic necessities listed in the annex) [[cgi:art. 128]].

- Taxable event: delivery, performance of the service, import [[cgi:art. 133]].
- Chargeability: at the taxable event for a delivery; an advance makes the tax chargeable on its amount [[cgi:art. 134]].
- Rates: standard 17.5%, reduced 10%, zero 0%; with 10% council cents, overall rate of [[param:tva.taux_global]] [[cgi:art. 142]].

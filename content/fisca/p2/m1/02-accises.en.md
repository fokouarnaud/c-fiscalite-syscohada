## A tax on certain products

Excise duties are charged on certain goods (beverages, tobacco, luxury products…) on top of VAT. Their rates are set by section 142 [[cgi:art. 142]]:

| Rate | Level |
|---|---|
| Very high | 50% |
| High | 30% |
| Standard | 25% |
| Medium | 12.5% |
| Reduced | 5% |
| Very reduced | 2% |

Excise duties carry council cents of **5%** (not 10% as for VAT) [[cgi:art. C 83]].

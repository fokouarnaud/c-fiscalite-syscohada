## Proportional, degressive or fixed

Registration gives a deed a certain date and triggers duties [[cgi:art. 543]] [[cgi:art. 544]] [[cgi:art. 545]]:

- **Proportional duties**, as a percentage of value: for example the intermediate rate of **10%** for transfers of developed urban property and leases of a business; a very reduced 1% rate for other deeds.
- **Degressive duties** on capital contributions (formation and capital increase of companies), by bracket.
- **Fixed duties**, regardless of value: 50,000 or 20,000 CFA francs depending on the deed.

Example: Mbarga Distribution buys a developed urban warehouse for 50,000,000 CFA francs; proportional duties of 10%: 5,000,000 CFA francs.

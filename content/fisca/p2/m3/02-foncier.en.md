## Property tax

Due every year on real property located in a municipality [[cgi:art. C 49]], it is charged at **0.1%** of the property's value; a progressive scale applies above 500,000,000 CFA francs [[cgi:art. C 53]].

## Local development tax (TDL)

Collected in return for basic services (street lighting, sanitation, refuse collection), it is withheld from salaries using a yearly scale by bracket of monthly base salary: from 3,000 CFA francs a year (salary of 62,000 to 75,000) to 30,000 a year (salary above 500,000) [[cgi:art. C 86]].

## Property transfer duties

Municipalities collect a property transfer duty of **10%** on deeds and transfers of developed urban property [[cgi:art. C 57]].

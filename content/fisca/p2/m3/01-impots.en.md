## For local authorities

Local taxation covers levies collected for municipalities, city councils and regions [[cgi:art. C 2]]. State tax services administer them and keep **10%** for assessment and collection costs [[cgi:art. C 4]].

Municipal taxes include in particular [[cgi:art. C 7]]:

- the business licence contribution (patente), based on turnover;
- the liquor licence contribution;
- the General Synthetic Tax (IGS);
- property tax;
- property transfer duties;
- the vehicle stamp duty.

On top come additional council cents on State taxes: 10% on personal income tax, corporate tax and VAT; 5% on excise duties [[cgi:art. C 83]].
